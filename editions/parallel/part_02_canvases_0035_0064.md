Page 62 (recto, numbered '62' in upper-right margin). Column A details the hands-on clinical examination before cataract couching: pressing the thumb onto the upper eyelid over the pupil and releasing quickly to check pupillary dilation/contraction and cataract mobility, inspecting obliquely for white adhesive threads (synechiae) binding the cataract to the cornea or uvea, and condemning unscrupulous practitioners who apply corrosive 'atz' (vitriol, alum, green stone, burnt stone) that ruin the cornea. Column B finishes that condemnation, presents Portrait No. 5 ('Figur des bösen Mangel der augen', showing constricted/irregular pupils), and opens 'Es ist auch ein andere ardt des Starn' describing congenital/innate constricted pupils. Transcribed with Gemini 4.
