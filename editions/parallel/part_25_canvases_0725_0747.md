## Page 48 — IIIF Canvas #727

**Folio / Side:** recto · **Image:** `b18775275_MS_990_0727.JP2` (2401 × 3206 px) · [IIIF Canvas](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/presentation/b18775275/canvases/b18775275_MS_990_0727.JP2) · [Full-Resolution Image](https://iiif.wellcomecollection.org/image/b18775275_MS_990_0727.JP2/full/full/0/default.jpg) · **Transcription Model:** Gemini 4 Hydrogen · **Tokens (In / Out):** 1,850 / 2,200

Page 48 recto contains an anatomical treatise and watercolor flap diagram (Klappbild) of the human eye ('Anatomia oder Zerlegung des Augs'), shown here with the outer anatomical flap closed (displaying the sclera/conjunctiva F and iris/pupil H). Column 1 presents the watercolor eye diagrams, muscle/tunic key, and a rubricated list of the seven eye membranes; Column 2 provides the rubricated title, anatomical key A–R, and prose explanations of the origins of the eye's membranes.

### Illustrations and Anatomical Diagrams

**Eye with extraocular muscles (labeled A)** (`#xywh=400,400,500,1000`): Top-left watercolor diagram of the eyeball with attached extraocular muscles, labeled A on the globe.

**Splayed extraocular muscles and optic nerve (labeled A–I, a)** (`#xywh=400,400,500,1000`): Top-right watercolor diagram within Column 1 showing the splayed eye muscles and vessels radiating from the central eyeball/optic nerve, labeled A, B, C, D, E, H, I, and a.

**Crystalline humor with aranea membrane (labeled B)** (`#xywh=400,400,500,1000`): Small circular watercolor diagram on the left showing the crystalline lens with the aranea membrane, labeled B.

**Crystalline humor alone (labeled D)** (`#xywh=400,400,500,1000`): Small orange-tinted circular watercolor diagram below B showing the crystalline humor alone, labeled D.

**Vitreous and crystalline humor (labeled C)** (`#xywh=400,400,500,1000`): Small semicircular watercolor diagram to the right of the main eye diagram showing the vitreous humor and crystalline lens, labeled C.

**Layered anatomical flap diagram of the eye (closed state, labeled B, F, H)** (`#xywh=400,400,500,1000`): Large central watercolor flap diagram (Klappbild) in Column 1 with the outermost flap closed, depicting the white sclera/conjunctiva with red blood vessels (labeled F), the optic nerve/muscle attachment at the top (labeled B), and the iris/pupil at the bottom (labeled H).
