Page 240 (recto) continues the discussion of stomach ailments, addressing cold phlegmatic accumulation, sympathetic illness from the liver, dietary excesses, and the difficulty of curing chronic cold stomach conditions. It recommends emetics (blessed water, English salt, Tartarum Emeticum) followed by a herbal decoction of mint, betony, calamus, angelica, and mastic. The second half of the page discusses the causes of stomach weakness ('Die Schwachheit des Magen khumbt Von'), particularly overeating and excessive purging.

Page 241 (verso) presents treatments for weakness of the stomach ('Cur in Schwacheit des Magn'), beginning with internal preparatory syrups (syrup of violets and roses with lentisk or tamarind), purgative pills of rhubarb and aloes, and a cooling decoction for the hot liver. It then gives multiple external recipes: fomentations ('Fumonda') with aromatic herbs in red wine or sour water, strengthening plasters with labdanum, aloes, and expressed nutmeg oil, three variations of herbal stomach bags ('Magn Säckhel'), and a warming stomach ointment ('Salbe') for a cold, weak stomach.
