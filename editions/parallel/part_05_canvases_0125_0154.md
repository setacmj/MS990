Page 154 (recto), two columns, no illustrations. Left column contains ophthalmic remedies for holding back eye fluxes (hot hard-boiled egg on the neck), obstructed eye nerves (tormentil plaster), foreign bodies in the eye (eye-pearls), pain and inflammation (roasted apple, egg-white, and Armenian bole salve), dim eyes (fennel herb), and sight lost from smallpox (pimpernel juice). Page 183 (folio verso) contains Early New High German and Latin medical recipes for external and internal fever remedies ('Etliche Euserliche mittel Wider das fieber'), including foot plasters, sympathetic/magical remedies using a spider in a nutshell and plantain roots tied to the pulse or neck, cinquefoil leaves, celandine, ordinary fever drinks, St. Ambrose's fever potion, fever potions with blessed thistle and theriac water, and a series of fever powders and elderberry/celandine remedies. Illustrated with a pointing manicule in the upper left margin and a shaded ink-and-wash pitcher/jug beside the ordinary fever drink.
