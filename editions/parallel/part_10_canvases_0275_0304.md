Page 303 (recto) transitions from melancholy and blood purification to diseases of the spleen. The left column features an anatomical watercolor illustration of a spleen with its branching vessels, followed by a discussion of the shared remedies and physiological relationship between the liver and spleen, the obstruction of the portal vein, and the spleen's office of drawing melancholic humor. The right column describes symptoms of splenic illness (flatulence, vertigo, tinnitus, diaphragm oppression, chills, quartan fevers, inflammation) and their causes (over-eating, cold slimy food, drinking in heat, studying after meals).
